What is a Fidelity Cash Management Account?
It's a brokerage account that's designed to help your savings grow at Fidelity with competitive rates on cash and everything you need for convenient spending and saving.
Here are some things you can do with this account:
Spend and withdraw money easily with a debit card
Pay bills and write checks
Add money via direct deposit with a designated account and routing number or by using mobile check deposit
Transfer money instantly between your eligible Fidelity accounts
Wire money for free
Who can open a Fidelity Cash Management Account?
It’s available exclusively to Fidelity customers. If you have any Fidelity account, you’re eligible to open a cash management account.
What’s the difference between a cash management account and other Fidelity accounts?
Fidelity Cash Management Accounts are designed to handle all your spending and savings needs. They are intended to complement your other accounts, not replace them. By pairing a cash management account with accounts that are designed for long-term savings or investments, like eligible retirement accounts or Fidelity Brokerage Accounts, you can set up regular transfers between accounts to easily invest excess cash as you see fit.
Can I invest using my Fidelity Cash Management Account?
Yes. To support your savings goals, the Fidelity Cash Management Account offers access to a variety of investment options. If your account was opened on or after September 28, 2026, available investments are limited to fixed income products, such as bonds and CDs7, and Fidelity money market mutual funds.
How does a Fidelity Cash Management Account compare with a high-yield savings account?
While both accounts can generally help you earn higher interest rates than a traditional bank or checking account, one major difference between Fidelity Cash Management Accounts and high-yield savings accounts are available features and FDIC-insurance coverage options. The Fidelity Cash Management Account has features that aren’t always offered by high-yield savings accounts—like a debit card, checkwriting, and mobile deposit. This means you get easy access to your cash while your money can earn a competitive rate.
When it comes to FDIC insurance, banks that offer high-yield savings accounts are FDIC-insured. The Fidelity Cash Management Account is a brokerage account that gives you the option to put your money into our FDIC-Insured Deposit Sweep Program, but you can also put your money into available money market funds, which tend to earn a higher rate. Fidelity® Government Money Market Fund (SPAXX) is not covered by FDIC insurance and therefore is subject to market risk. However, SPAXX, along with all other securities in the Fidelity Cash Management Account, is covered by Securities Investor Protection Corporation (SIPC)—which protects up to $500,000 (including $250,000 for claims for cash). SIPC coverage does not protect against market losses, but it does protect against brokerage firm failure or bankruptcy. An explanatory brochure available upon request or at www.SIPC.org.
How does a Fidelity Cash Management Account compare with a checking account?
Both a cash management account and a traditional bank checking account may offer some of the same features like bill pay, debit card, and mobile deposit—however, there are often a few key differences in earning potential, FDIC insurance, and fees. The Fidelity Cash Management account offers multiple ways to earn with our FDIC-insured Deposit Sweep Program or through money market funds that are invested in low-risk securities, like SPAXX. SPAXX is not covered by FDIC insurance and may have a higher earning potential than traditional bank checking accounts. Additionally, some banks require minimum balances to avoid fees, and they don't all reimburse ATM fees at machines outside of their bank's network. The Fidelity Cash Management Account offers unlimited ATM reimbursements, and there’s no account fees or minimum balances.
