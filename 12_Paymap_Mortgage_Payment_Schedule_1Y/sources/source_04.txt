Peace of mind with flexible automatic payments
Enroll now
With Chase automatic payments from your checking account, you choose the option that works best for you and your budget. It is a great way to help ensure your payment is applied correctly and at the time you choose. We will even update your mortgage payment if it changes due to escrow or adjustable rate changes.
Choose the option that works best for you:
Once a month
Mortgage accounts: You can choose any payment date within your grace period
Home equity line of credit accounts: Your payment will be made on your due date
Payment is made on the same day each month
Ensures correct payment amount
Enroll now
Twice a month
Available for mortgage loans only
Splits monthly payments into two half payments
You choose the payment dates
Great for sticking to a budget
Half payments are held as “unapplied funds” until a full payment is received
Enroll now
Every two weeks
Available for mortgage loans only
During your first month of enrollment, you'll need to make both your regular payment plus your two half payments
Pay your loan down faster
Splits your monthly payment into two half payments
Half payments are held as "unapplied funds" until a full payment is received
There are usually two months per year where you’ll make an additional half payment. These two extra half payments are applied to your principal, adding up to one full extra payment each year.

Chase Home Lending
We offer a variety of mortgages for buying a new home or refinancing your existing one. New to homebuying? Our Learning Center provides easy-to-use mortgage calculators, educational articles and more. Our ultimate guide for first-time homebuyers gives an overview of the process from start to finish. And from applying for a loan to managing your mortgage, Chase MyHome has everything you need.
Buying a House
Whether you're determining how much house you can afford, estimating your monthly payment with our mortgage calculator or looking to get preapproved for a mortgage, we can help you at any part of the home buying process. See our current mortgage rates, low down payment options, and jumbo mortgage loans.
Refinance
Refinance your existing mortgage to lower your monthly payments, pay off your loan sooner, or access cash for a large purchase. Use our home value estimator to estimate the current value of your home. See our current refinance rates and compare refinance options.
Affordability
Our affordable lending options, including FHA loans and VA loans, help make homeownership possible. Check out our affordability calculator, and look for homebuyer grants in your area. Visit our mortgage education center for helpful tips and information. And from applying for a loan to managing your mortgage, Chase MyHome has you covered.
HELOC
A home equity line of credit (HELOC) lets you access your home's equity to pay for renovations, consolidate debt or handle major expenses. See how a Chase HELOC works and discover the benefits of putting your home's value to work for you. Use our HELOC calculator to estimate your available credit, compare a HELOC to a home equity loan, and review estimated HELOC rates. Learn how the draw and repayment periods work, then see how to apply. Manage your account anytime with Chase MyHome.
