Wells Fargo Active Cash vs Wells Fargo Autograph: Which no-annual-fee card is more valuable?
Either card is a great choice, but if you're a traveler there's a clear winner.

FAQs
Annual fee
Neither the Wells Fargo Autograph® Card nor the Wells Fargo Active Cash® Card has an annual fee, and both offer a generous intro APR on purchases.
The intro APR you receive with the Active Cash also applies to qualifying balance transfers. However, if that's what you're looking for, there are balance transfer credit cards with much longer intro-APR periods.
Winner: Tie.
Welcome bonus
The Wells Fargo Active Cash® Card offers $100 cash rewards bonus after spending $500 in purchases in the first 3 months. The Wells Fargo Autograph® Card earns points instead of cash rewards. You'll earn 20,000 bonus points after spending $1,000 in purchases in the first three months from opening the account.
You can redeem the points you earn with the Autograph Card for a $200 cash value. However, you also have the option to transfer those points to airline and hotel loyalty programs, which can dramatically increase their value. While the Autograph Card's bonus is more flexible and valuable, it has a higher spending requirement, so the Active Cash Card's bonus could make more sense for you.
Winner: Wells Fargo Autograph Card.
Rewards
The Wells Fargo Autograph® Card is a travel credit card that earns flexible points and the Wells Fargo Active Cash® Card is a flat-rate 2% cash rewards credit card.

The Active Cash Card has no bonus categories to consider and earns twice as much value as the Autograph on purchases that don't earn bonus rewards. However, the Autograph Card has a strong list of spending categories that earn 3X points (50% more).
Either card can be the most rewarding. It comes down to where you spend the most.

Redemption options
You can redeem the rewards you earn with either card in the following ways:
Gift cards
Statement credits
Direct deposit into an eligible Wells Fargo account
ATM withdrawal with a Wells Fargo ATM or debit card ($20 increments)
Pay with rewards at checkout with PayPal
Book travel through Wells Fargo
You'll usually receive one cent per point for these redemptions. One cent in cash rewards is equal to one Wells Fargo rewards point. So a $25 gift card or $25 statement credit costs 2,500 points.
However, the Autograph Card has an additional feature that sets it apart—it allows you to transfer points to travel loyalty programs. You can transfer Wells Fargo points (1:1 ratio for most partners) to the following airline and hotel programs:
Aer Lingus AerClub
Avianca LifeMiles
British Airways Executive Club
Flying Blue (Air France and KLM)
Iberia Plus
Choice Privileges (1:2 ratio)
Virgin Red (Virgin Atlantic)
This expands how you can redeem points and unlocks the potential to receive much more value for your points. For example, at the time of writing, you can book the Clarion Hotel The Hub in Oslo for 15,000 Choice points (7,500 Wells Fargo points at the 1:2 transfer ratio) on a weekend during peak travel season for Norway. That's a stay that costs about $428 (kr 4,499), which gives your points a value of 5.7 cents per point.
Another benefit is that if you have both the Active Cash and Autograph Cards, you can transfer the rewards you earn with the Active Cash Card to airlines and hotels.
Winner: Wells Fargo Autograph Card.

Which should you get?
The Wells Fargo Autograph® Card is the clear choice for anyone who wants to use credit card rewards to travel. You'll earn more on travel purchases and have far more options for high-value travel redemptions.
The Wells Fargo Active Cash® Card is a straightforward cash rewards credit card. You won't need to remember bonus spending categories because it offers a great return on transactions. It's especially valuable if most of your expenses fall outside the Autograph Card's bonus categories, where it earns twice as much.
If you currently have one card, there is a strong argument that you could benefit from having the other. The bonus spending categories complement each other, so having both cards can help you maximize your return on spending. Plus, having the Autograph Card allows you to transfer the rewards you earn with the Active Cash to all of Wells Fargo's travel partners.

FAQs
Does Wells Fargo have transfer partners?
Yes, in 2024 Wells Fargo added airline and hotel transfer partners to its rewards program. However, only certain cards have access to these transfer partners.
Is the Wells Fargo Autograph Card hard to get?
The Wells Fargo Autograph Card requires a good to excellent credit score to qualify. That's a FICO Score of 670+, so it can be hard to get if you are rebuilding your credit.
What are the drawbacks of the Wells Fargo Active Cash Card?
The Wells Fargo Active Cash Card is an all-around solid rewards credit card, with few weaknesses. However, you can't transfer its rewards to travel partners unless you pair it with the Wells Fargo Autograph® Card or Wells Fargo Autograph Journey® Card.
